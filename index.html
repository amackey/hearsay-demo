An idea I'm kicking around — I'd love your gut reaction.

Hearsay tells you about a handful of concerts near you that you'd actually want to go to — including bands you've never heard of — and says in plain English why it picked each one.

It knows what music you like, which venues you enjoy, and whether you're already busy that night. Then it sends five shows a week. Not five hundred.

It also tries to be honest about the things apps usually hide — what it can't see, when a ticket is over your budget, and when a great show is a bad Tuesday.

None of this is real yet — it's a clickable sketch. The venues are real Philadelphia rooms; the bands are invented. Tap things, most of it responds. Pick your own city up there — the rooms change with it.

You're on a big screen, so it's shown inside a phone. Open this same file on your phone and it fills the screen like a real app.
